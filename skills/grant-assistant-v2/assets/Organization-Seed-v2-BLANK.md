## Core intake
1. Issue areas: What causes, sectors or problems does the organization address?
2. Geography: Where is the organization headquartered, and what places should grant discovery cover?
3. Beneficiaries: Which populations or communities does the organization serve?
4. Applicant type: What is its legal or organizational type (nonprofit, government, fiscally sponsored project, etc.)?
5. Fiscal sponsorship (conditional): Does the organization use a fiscal sponsor? If so, who, and what is the confirmed relationship?
6. Federal registration (conditional): Should federal opportunities be searched? If yes, what are the known SAM.gov registration and UEI statuses? Do not paste sensitive credentials.
7. History: When was the organization founded, and what audited financial years, if any, are available?
8. Budget: What is the approximate annual operating budget and currency? Unknown is acceptable; budget-dependent conclusions may be deferred.
9. Funding preferences: Which funding types are acceptable or excluded? Loans are excluded by default unless explicitly approved.
10. Restricted funders (optional, repeatable): Which funders are restricted, why, and until when?
11. Partners (optional, repeatable): Which organizations might participate, and in what proposed roles? Do not presume consent.
12. Other eligibility barriers (optional): What rules or constraints commonly make the organization ineligible?
13. Programs and maturity: What programs or projects exist, and are they planned, pilot, established, or expanding?
14. Preferred award range (optional): What minimum and maximum award sizes are realistic? Proposed ranges require confirmation.
15. Additional discovery terms (optional): Which search terms or opportunity categories should be included?

## Confirmation and safe installation
- Assistant summarizes the answers and distinguishes confirmed information from unknowns and candidate extractions.
- Assistant maps approved answers to Organization Profiles using the versioned 84-field v2 contract; repeatable restrictions and partner roles need separate review before writing.
- Assistant shows destination, expected four collections, any existing installation conflicts, and required permissions.
- User explicitly approves organization seed and storage creation/reconciliation before any write.
- After readback, installer binds and generates five skills; actual import/discovery and authenticated execution must be verified separately.
- No grants, outcomes, stage promotions, or operator decisions are invented during installation.
